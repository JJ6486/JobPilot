# What to return
1. `jd`: parse the JD.
   - role_family: "software_ict" for software / IT / ICT engineering or technical roles; "teaching" for teaching or training roles (including ICT, computer science and maths teachers); "other" otherwise.
   - must_have_skills / nice_to_have_skills: short names of skills, tools, qualifications or experience types. Items under "required", "must", "qualifications" are must-haves; "plus", "preferred", "nice to have", "extra points" are nice-to-haves.
   - hard_conditions: only truly non-negotiable conditions (nationality, visa/iqama transfer, gender, a mandatory licence or teaching qualification, required location without remote option, driving licence). Not ordinary skills.
   - remote_allowed: true only if the JD says remote or hybrid-from-anywhere is possible.
   - Leave unknown fields empty. Copy contact details exactly as written.
2. `requirements`: one judgement per must-have and nice-to-have item.
   - "matched": the truth bank has this skill at level core or project (by name or alias), or a bullet/project clearly proves it. `evidence_ids` MUST list real ids from the truth bank (bullet ids like exp_1_b2, project ids, workshop ids, education ids, certification ids). Set matched_skill to the truth-bank skill name if one applies.
   - "adjacent": the candidate lacks it but has a closely related skill (it appears in that skill's adjacent_to, or is an obvious sibling, e.g. Vue vs React). Put the related skill in matched_skill and its evidence in evidence_ids.
   - "missing": anything else, including skills that are only at level "learning" and anything listed in never_claim.
   - Degree requirements: matched only if the truth-bank education satisfies it. Teaching qualifications (B.Ed, PGCE, teaching licence, TESOL/CELTA as a teaching credential) are missing unless the truth bank lists one.
3. `relevant_years`: years of professional experience relevant to THIS role, computed from experience start/end dates (end "Present" = today). For teaching roles, count only actual teaching employment; workshops, mentoring and training sessions are related experience but are not years of classroom teaching. Be strict.
   `experience_fit`: strong / partial / weak versus the JD's level and years. Explain in experience_note.
4. `role_alignment`: strong / partial / weak: how close the candidate's actual roles and titles are to this job. Explain in role_alignment_note.
5. `hard_conditions`: judge each parsed hard condition against constraints, education, certifications and never_claim. "not_met" when the candidate clearly does not satisfy it; "unclear" when the truth bank can't tell. Give a one-sentence reason.
6. `logistics`: location (accepted_cities, open_to_relocation, open_to_remote vs the job's city and remote_allowed), visa/iqama, licence, language, schedule. Status ok / concern / blocker.
7. `red_flags`: short phrases for anything worrying (e.g. "requires 5+ years, candidate has ~3", "Saudi nationals only").
8. `summary`: exactly three short lines (max ~20 words each): overall fit, biggest strength, biggest gap or risk.
